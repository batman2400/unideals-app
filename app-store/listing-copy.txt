Description (4000)
Uni Deals is the student discount app for Sri Lanka.

Verified students unlock partner offers: online promo codes and in-store QR tickets. Create an account, verify with your university email or student ID, then browse deals and campus events.

Partners publish offers and scan student tickets at the register. There are no in-app purchases.

On iPhone, sign in with email and password. If you already created an account with Google on the website or Android, use Forgot password with the same email to set a password.

Uni Deals is for ages 13 and older. It is not designed for children under 13.
